Delete your data from Living Postcards

Living Postcards by Eggs' Labs · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Living Postcards has no accounts, and we don't keep a copy of your progress. Your data is on your device, in your device backup, with Google (ads), or with Google Play or the App Store (purchases). Here is how to delete each one.

1. Your progress, coins and settings

This deletes your progress, coins, stars, settings, your age answer and the game's random install ID.

  • Android: uninstall the game, or open Settings → Apps → Living Postcards → Storage → Clear data.
  • iPhone and iPad: touch and hold the game's icon, then Remove App → Delete App. ("Offload App" keeps your data.)

Deleting this cannot be undone.

2. The copy in your device backup

If device backup is on, a copy may also be in your Google or iCloud backup, and it comes back if you reinstall.

  • Android: delete the device backup in your Google account (Google One or Google Drive → Backups).
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Backups, then turn off Living Postcards or delete the backup.

3. Ads data (held by Google)

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Manage or delete ad activity linked to your Google account at myadcenter.google.com and myactivity.google.com.

We can't delete this data for you, because Google holds it, not us. Google keeps it under its own retention policy.

4. Purchases

Google Play or the App Store keeps your order history, including what tax and accounting law requires. Deleting the game doesn't remove a purchase: Remove ads can be restored from the game's Shop on the same Google or Apple account. To ask about your order records, use Google's or Apple's privacy tools.

5. Emails you sent us, and any other request

Email games@simplifiedai.io with "Delete my data" in the subject, from the address you wrote to us from. We delete our emails with you within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

Full details are in our privacy policy.
